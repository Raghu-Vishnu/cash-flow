Privacy policy

Cash Flow, a personal income and expense tracker · Last updated 17 November 2026

In short: Cash Flow has no server. Your bank statements and transactions stay on your own device. If you choose to back up to Google Drive, the backup goes straight from your browser to your own Google Drive. The people who make Cash Flow never receive, see or store your data.

1. Data the app handles

  • Bank statements you upload (CSV, Excel or PDF) are read inside your browser. The files are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Your transactions, people, accounts, categories and settings are saved in your browser's storage on that device only.
  • The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

2. Optional Google Drive backup

Backing up to Google Drive is optional. You only use it if you click Back up to Drive or Restore from Drive in Settings.

  • Permission requested: only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This lets the app see, create and update only the files it created itself. In Cash Flow, that is one file named "Cash Flow backup.json". The app cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • What is saved: a copy of your Cash Flow data (the same content as Back up (JSON) in Settings), stored in your own Google Drive.
  • How it travels: directly between your browser and Google over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. It does not pass through any server run by Cash Flow.
  • Your email address: read from Google only to show "Connected to …" on your device. It is stored only in your browser and never sent to us.
  • Sign-in token: Google's access token is kept in memory for about one hour and is never saved or shared.
  • The app never deletes files in your Drive.

Cash Flow's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to save and restore your own backup. It is never sold, never used for advertising, never shared with anyone, and never read by people.

3. Third-party services

  • GitHub Pages hosts the app's files. Like any website host, GitHub may log basic technical information, such as IP addresses, when the app loads. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts provides the app's fonts and icons. Google receives a standard web request when they load. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Google sign-in and Google Drive are used only for the optional backup, under Google's own privacy policy.

4. Your control over your data

  • Delete data on a device: Settings → Delete all data, or clear the browser's site data.
  • Disconnect Google Drive: Settings → Google Drive backup → Disconnect. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the backup: delete "Cash Flow backup.json" from your Google Drive.

5. Children

Cash Flow is not directed at children under 13.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
