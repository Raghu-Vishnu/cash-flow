Privacy policy

Cash Flow, a personal income and expense tracker · Last updated 17 November 2026

In short: Cash Flow has no server. Your transactions are saved in your own Google Drive, and on the devices where you choose "Remember this device". Data goes straight between your browser and Google. The people who make Cash Flow never receive, see or store your data.

1. Data the app handles

  • Bank statements you upload (CSV, Excel or PDF) are read inside your browser. The files are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Your transactions, people, accounts, categories and settings are saved in your own Google Drive (see below). On a device where you choose Remember this device, a copy is also kept in the browser's storage so the app opens quickly and works offline. On other devices, the browser removes it when you close the tab.
  • Downloads (CSV, Excel, PDF) are created on your device and saved where you choose. They are not uploaded anywhere.
  • The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

2. Google sign-in and Google Drive

You sign in with your Google account when you first open Cash Flow on a device. Sign-in keeps each person's data separate and saves it in your own Google Drive, so it stays in step across your devices. Sign-in happens in your browser with Google. There is no Cash Flow account database, and no server receives your details.

  • Permissions requested: openid, email and profile (your Google account ID, email address and name), and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. drive.file lets the app see, create, update and delete only the files it created itself. In Cash Flow, that is one file named "Cash Flow backup.json", which holds your Cash Flow data. The app cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • Your name, email address and Google account ID are stored only in your browser on that device. They are used to show who is signed in and to keep each account's data separate. They are never sent to us.
  • What is saved: your Cash Flow data, updated automatically after each change while you are signed in.
  • How it travels: directly between your browser and Google over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. It does not pass through any server run by Cash Flow.
  • Sign-in token: Google's access token is kept in memory for about one hour and is never saved or shared.
  • Deleting: the app deletes its file only when you choose Delete Drive copy, or when you choose to delete the old copy after changing Google account. Deleted backups go to your Drive Bin, where you can restore them for 30 days. The app never touches any other file.

Cash Flow's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to sign you in and to save, load and delete your own Cash Flow file. It is never sold, never used for advertising, never shared with anyone, and never read by people.

3. Third-party services

  • GitHub Pages hosts the app's files. Like any website host, GitHub may log basic technical information, such as IP addresses, when the app loads. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts provides the app's fonts and icons. Google receives a standard web request when they load. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Google sign-in and Google Drive are used for sign-in and backup, under Google's own privacy policy.

4. Your control over your data

  • Delete data on a device: Settings → Delete all data, or clear the browser's site data.
  • Sign out: Settings → Google account & backup → Sign out. You can also remove the data from that device as you sign out.
  • Change Google account: Settings → Change Google account moves your data to another of your Google accounts.
  • Remove Cash Flow's access to your Google account: at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the Drive copy: Settings → Delete Drive copy, or delete "Cash Flow backup.json" from your Google Drive yourself.
  • Stop keeping data on a device: Settings → turn off Remember this device.

5. Children

Cash Flow is not directed at children under 13.
